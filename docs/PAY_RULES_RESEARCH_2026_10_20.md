# Canadian pay rules source review

Reviewed 2026-10-20 for PR #54. This review covers general overtime, vacation pay and statutory holidays for private sector employees in all 13 provinces and territories and federally regulated workplaces. It does not certify every occupational exemption or collective agreement. Those require explicit employee and employer facts.

## Interpretation and implementation requirements

* Review dates are not enactment dates. Preserve known effective dates, notably PEI on 2026-06-30. Do not back apply current rules to historical work.
* Province for income tax does not establish federal or provincial employment jurisdiction. A federally regulated employer follows federal labour rules.
* A payroll week, workday, regular schedule, holiday eligibility, substituted holiday, averaging agreement and service anniversary are inputs, not facts inferable from a province code.
* Keep vacation pay accrued separate from paid wages and deductions. Wage bases differ. Alberta and Manitoba exclude overtime wages. Other bases can include holiday pay, past vacation pay, tips, commissions or statutory meals and lodging values.
* Holiday dates and holiday pay entitlement are different. Never pay every named date without eligibility and substitution inputs. Never infer a holiday rate from the ordinary overtime rate.
* Source access: Ontario guide requests returned 403, but indexed official pages and the official policy manual were available. B.C. interpretive pages intermittently returned 502; the current official Act was available. NWT HTML timed out; its official FAQ PDF was available. Quebec, Yukon and federal sources were successfully checked.

## Minimum wage history used by NB and NL overtime floors

The implementation retains the regular contracted hourly wage as a floor and applies the higher statutory minimum overtime wage. A higher contracted overtime rate must be supplied separately; the statutory minimum is not an assertion about every contract.
